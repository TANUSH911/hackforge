## Patterns across winners
- Winners pair a narrow user (rural patients, dementia carers, dyscalculia pupils) with a working demo.
- Offline, voice and local-language support recur (Aarogyamitra, Nana Shilpa, NeoScreen).
- Hardware plus app (ExoHeal, Gluco Pulse) stands out against app-only entries.
- Clinician-set problems (AIIMS, ministry statements) are rewarded over generic wellness.
- Sponsor-tech fit counts: Gemini for VAYU, Azure for Imagine Cup.
- Mood trackers and generic wellness chatbots are everywhere; Epione won by adding SOS, doctors and a ministry match.
- No confirmed winner was a plain symptom-checker chatbot.
## Idea seeds for a solo 24h build
- Offline-first, voice-led triage in a regional language for an ASHA worker.
- Caregiver tool for dementia: wandering alert plus a simple "bring them home" flow.
- Phone-camera screening for one condition, running on-device, with a clinician hand-off summary.
- Pre-visit intake agent that turns a patient's story into a structured note for a doctor.
- Student crisis companion with an SOS contact flow and consent-based location sharing.
