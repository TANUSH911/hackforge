# Mock hackathon 02 — HealthHack Bengaluru (24h, in person)

## Problem statements (pick one)
1. **Medication adherence for the elderly.** Many older adults in Indian cities live alone or with working children and miss doses of daily medicines for BP, diabetes or thyroid. Build a solution that helps patients take medicines correctly and lets family or doctors know when something goes wrong.
2. **Smarter primary-care queues.** Government PHCs and small clinics in Bengaluru see long, unpredictable waits. Build a solution that reduces waiting time or helps staff prioritise patients.
3. **Open innovation in community health.** Any solution that measurably improves health for an underserved community.

## Judging criteria
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Problem understanding & impact | 25% |
| Innovation | 20% |
| Technical execution (working demo) | 30% |
| User experience & accessibility | 15% |
| Pitch | 10% |

## Rules
- 24 hours. Solo or teams up to 4. Open-source libraries and free APIs allowed.
- Do not use real patient data. Synthetic data must be labelled.
- Submission: GitHub repo, 3-minute demo video, live link if possible, 6-slide deck.

## Sponsor tracks
- **Best use of Generative AI**
- **Most accessible design** (elderly / low-literacy / regional language users)

## Builder
Solo, intermediate Python/Java/C, ₹0 budget, 8 GB Intel MacBook.
